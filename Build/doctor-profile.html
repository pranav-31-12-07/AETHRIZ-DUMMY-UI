About Dr. Sarah Lin

Dr. Sarah Lin is a board-certified cardiologist specializing in preventive cardiology, heart failure management, and advanced echocardiography. She received her medical degree from Stanford University School of Medicine and completed her fellowship at the University of California, San Francisco (UCSF).

Her clinical philosophy blends evidence-based lipid management, continuous sensor-driven biomarker tracking (wearable ECG & heart rate variability), and proactive lifestyle interventions to optimize long-term cardiovascular longevity.
